The Datasets — 21 CSVs, 20 Traps

Every assignment challenge ships with a synthetic dataset that contains the failure mode. Fictional, seeded, classroom-safe. Download one, load it in Power BI or your HTML stack, and grill it before you visualise it.

20 Challenges CoveredSeeded & ReproducibleFictional Data OnlyPower BI · CSV
From the Desk of Puneet Arora, Aplly.xyz Trainer

📦 How to Use These Files

  • Download: click a CSV button below (opens the raw file on GitHub — save it), or grab the whole folder from GitHub, or clone the repo.
  • Power BI: Get Data → Text/CSV. HTML: fetch or inline the CSV. Spreadsheets: open directly — but watch Challenge 8's row count.
  • Trap warning: each card shows the embedded trap. Do not read it if you want to discover it yourself — finding it during the grill is worth marks.
  • Rules: no fabricating rows, no hand-editing CSVs (documented transformations only). Details in datasets/README.md.

🌱 Easy Tier

1

The Hero Axis

Easy CSV · 24 rows
Columns: month, attendance_pct — 24 months, all values 85–96%.
Trap: no zero in range. Every y-axis start hides or exaggerates something.
2

Red Means Go?

Easy CSV · 16 rows
Columns: equipment_id, equipment_name, location, status, checked_at — status ∈ in_use / faulty / available.
Trap: status is text only. A green/red board needs shape or label redundancy to survive colour-blindness and grayscale.
3

The Missing Tuesday

Easy CSV · 30 rows
Columns: date, steps, sensor_status — two days have empty steps, sensor_status=outage.
Trap: naive imports turn empty into 0 — "nobody walked on Tuesday". Show a gap, not a dip.
4

Pie Overload

Easy CSV · 14 rows
Columns: category, amount_inr — 14 budget categories.
Trap: 14 slices, no comparison. Grill your audience down to the 3 categories they actually decide on.
5

The Zoomed Breakout

Easy CSV · 156 rows
Columns: week_starting, active_members — 3 years, last 3 weeks jump.
Trap: zoom to the tail and you "prove" explosive growth from flat history. Show the full range with the zoom annotated.
6

Which Average?

Easy CSV · 38 rows
Columns: roll_no, student_name, section, marks — mean ≈ 68, median ≈ 64.
Trap: right-skewed. One badged "average" misleads the story; your tooltip must say which mean.

⚡ Medium Tier

7

Double-Ruler Drama

Medium CSV · 24 rows
Columns: month, bus_ontime_pct, diesel_cost_inr_thousands — % drifts down, ₹ drifts up.
Trap: dual axes will make the lines cross — inviting a causation claim the data never made.
8

Silent Row Loss

Medium CSV · 1,200 rows
Columns: record_id, collection_date, block, weight_kg, students_involved
Trap: crosses the classic 1,000-row import cap. Assert row counts at every hop — capture → import → dashboard.
9

Hue-Only Heat Map

Medium CSV · 96 rows
Columns: room, hour_slot, occupancy_level_0_to_3, approx_occupants — 8 rooms × 12 hours.
Trap: one-blue-intensity encoding dies in grayscale. Redundancy: numbers, labels, hatch.
10

The Flicker Feed

Medium CSV · 900 rows
Columns: timestamp, counter_id, queue_length — one row per second, 15 minutes.
Trap: repaint on every ping = unreadable flicker. Choose an aggregation window and a repaint cap.
11

Mobile Mashup

Medium CSV · 60 rows
Columns: roll_no, student_name, section, gender + six subject scores (maths…computer).
Trap: six "panels" of data designed for a lab monitor — decide the ONE question a phone must answer.
12

Hover-Only Facts

Medium CSV · 24 rows
Columns: month, consumption — no unit column (values are kWh).
Trap: units and range that live only in tooltips vanish in a screenshot. Print them on the chart.
13

Two Vote Counts

Medium Sheet A · 75 Sheet B · 25
Columns: vote_id, student_id, project — sheet A: 3 votes/student; sheet B: 1 vote/student.
Trap: two "official" totals. Grill for the governed definition of a vote and who owns it.
14

Names on the Big Screen

Medium CSV · 50 rows
Columns: roll_no, student_name, section, gender, age + 3 events, composite, rank.
Trap: real-looking PII with per-student scores. Decide who sees whose number before you project it.

🔥 Hard Tier

15

Cluster Mirage

Hard CSV · 126 rows
Columns: student_id, study_hours_per_week, sports_hours_per_week — two engineered blobs + 6 bridge points.
Trap: pretty islands in 2D. What evidence beyond the projection confirms a "type"? Show the doubt.
16

Alarm Storm

Hard CSV · 60 rows
Columns: timestamp, zone, severity, message_code — ~20 alerts/min, only 3 are critical.
Trap: alert fatigue hides the real one. Severity tiering + a calm baseline screen.
17

The Sarcasm Meter

Hard CSV · 20 rows
Columns: feedback_id, comment, sentiment_score_0_to_1, label — sarcastic comments score ~0.9 "positive".
Trap: the meter confidently wrong. Carry uncertainty and show sample quotes next to every score.
18

Phantom Spike

Hard CSV · 302 rows
Columns: timestamp, vehicle_id, source, speed_kph — gps_live per-second + daily_log averages in one file.
Trap: mixed cadence collides on one chart into a spike that never happened. Reconcile clocks; label cadence.
19

Broken Inheritance

Hard CSV · 40 rows
Columns: year_label, event_date, event_name, category, recipient_first_name, position — label says 2024, dates span 2023–2026.
Trap: the hardcoded year poisons year-on-year comparison. Derive the year from the date; one source of truth.
20

Export Amnesia

Hard CSV · 80 rows
Columns: application_id, received_date, program, quota, entrance_score, interview_score, docs_verified, status, counselor_remarks
Trap: the truth hides in free-text row detail. Design every view to survive being flattened to an image.
Your submission PDF is itself a flattened dashboard (Challenge 20 is a free rehearsal). Whatever your dataset hides, the fix must be visible on paper.
